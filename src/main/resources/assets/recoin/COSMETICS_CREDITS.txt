Third-party content used by the Cosmetics module
================================================

All models below were converted to the client's formats (vanilla item models or the
cosmetic geo JSON in assets/recoin/cosmetics). Conversion changed the file format,
UV layout, placement and, for wings, the pose (flat feather panels, idle flap animation).
